The one T3 failure, at `K` and at the control, is `ones_gpu`:
`DriverError(CUDA_ERROR_NOT_FOUND, "named symbol not found")`, identical on
both sides. It is the expected sm_75 failure (requirements.md § 2): the BF16
and F8 fill kernels are built only for sm_80 and later
(`candle-kernels/src/fill.cu:76`), so the PTX built at 75 lacks the symbol.
Classified equal to the control; not a defect. Consequence for FathomDB
(R-FK-VERIFY F2): `fill_bf16`, `copy2d_bf16`, `const_set_bf16` and the F8
kernels are absent from any PTX built at `CUDA_COMPUTE_CAP=75`, which is
FathomDB's x86 release build, so with that build BF16 and F8 are
unavailable on every GPU, RTX 3090 included, not only on Turing. FathomDB
must never select BF16 or F8 with the cc 75 build. This follows from the
source (`candle-kernels/src/fill.cu:70-87`); K-G1 at 75 ran T1 and T2 only,
so it was not observed on the 3090.
